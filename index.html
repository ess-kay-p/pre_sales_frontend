Your Virtual Key: An Invitation to Indulgence

Each Solitaire client receives a hyper-personalised video message via WhatsApp featuring their name and Solitaire Status. The video shows a virtual vault or ornate digital door labeled with their Access Tier. As their name illuminates, a digital key is rendered, unlocking the exclusive app experience. The video ends with a custom QR or tappable link: 'Unlock your new Concierge world now, [Name].'
